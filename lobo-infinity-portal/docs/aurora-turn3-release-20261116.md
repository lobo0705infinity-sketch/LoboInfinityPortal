# Aurora VII: consolidated audit corrections and Turn 3 preparation

Approved by the commissioner; announced before play. Campaign policy `2026-11-15-audit` accompanies scoring revision `2026-11-balance`. The three new preparation mechanics begin with Turn 3; Turn 2 failure and resolved standings establish eligibility. Frozen historical Turns retain their recorded rules.

## Preparation procedure

1. Resolve the preceding Turn and all awards. Resolve existing territory exclusions and mandatory inclusions in their established order.
2. Create the next Turn’s nomination queue for the eight Conquest factions. Projected VP is earned Campaign VP plus printed value of current holdings. Sort lowest first, then fewer previous used nominations, then a persisted random draw. Each offered faction has 24 hours. On expiry, the next faction receives its own 24-hour window. Any eligible own, enemy or neutral territory can fill one remaining slot. Four mandatory selections skip nomination with no usage.
3. Finalize and publish all four vulnerable territories together, with the selection deadline, before operations or games. The GM fills the other slots. Public records show the nominated territory and nomination history; pending choices remain faction-private.
4. Prepare the Turn’s operations. For any of all eleven factions that failed an assigned objective in the preceding Turn, privately offer two eligible, comparably difficult objectives with different approaches and designate the default. A failed objective may be reissued; completed objectives cannot return. If only one remains, record that fact and assign it. At the six-VP cap there is no further objective award. Only the chosen option is assigned; an unanswered offer uses the designated default at the deadline. Success returns to normal assignment next Turn. Valid cap-excluded game evidence remains eligible under the objective’s wording.
5. Each Conquest faction with three or four owned vulnerable territories selects up to two owner advantages. One or two activate automatically. A missed deadline selects highest printed VP, then alphabetical territory name. Lock and publish the selections and frozen Turn-start owner identities. Every general battlefield rule remains active; existing benefit usage limits apply in every eligible game. Employment and support transfer no advantage.
6. Lock all objectives and operations before the global opening of games. This implements the commissioner’s explicit global timing decision. Opening is blocked until required options and owner selections are settled and published.

The registered commander or deputy can submit through Faction Decisions. Other registered faction members can read their own faction’s information. The GM can record an authorized nomination or objective choice received privately. No faction sees another faction’s options, requirements or pending nomination. Private target/contract data never enters public publication patches.

## Audit corrections

- Neutral opening in GM, Player and public rules; no starting packages or compensation.
- INSTALL accepts an uncontrolled target or another controller; PRESERVE requires the incumbent. Hidden Hand needs the declared result and at least five own counted target TP. A Tohaa employment contract requires that full success plus NA2’s own threshold.
- Captures include neutral claims. Express enemy-controller conditions remain. ALEPH uses its own control/highest-combined-counted-TP condition without an imported active-defense gate.
- Objectives require an exact-Turn recorded assignment; incomplete assignments expire at resolution, with close-of-reporting evidence review. Explicit later reissue retains history. Completed titles cannot be assigned again.
- Late reports never count under the new policy and cannot be approved. Reporting deadlines freeze when games open. Confirmation wording distinguishes verification from counted inclusion.
- Territory rules apply to all territorial missions; Narrative exceptions remain explicit. Complete Nova arrival timing, Azure cache placement and Sulfur exposure procedures are present online.
- Final ties use the official counted TP and OP for each Turn’s revision, then fixed public priority. Turn 6 Narrative gives two VP without an additional special reward. Private GM guide specifies all eleven default epilogues.
- Current Player, Terrain and Special Rules downloads have maintained URLs. Historical special-rules and terrain PDF routes redirect to the maintained guide. The GM guide stays private. Briefing source bodies contain the operative rules; no Discord messages are posted or edited.

## Validation

Isolated regression tests cover nomination ranking, reward precedence, expiry and history; owner selections, defaults and frozen publication; objective options, defaults, expiry and non-repeatability; registered representative authorization and faction privacy; strict late rejection; neutral claims, INSTALL, ALEPH and final counted ties. Existing Aurora reporting, practice, registration, publication and briefing checks remain release requirements. Tests do not create live campaign games or choices.
