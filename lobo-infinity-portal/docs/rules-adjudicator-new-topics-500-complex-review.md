# Questions Requiring Your Review

These 20 questions combine multiple rules or require interpretation. The other 480 answers are direct combinations of explicit rules and do not require review.

## 1. How do Camouflaged State and Suppressive Fire interact when a Marker wants to enter Suppressive Fire?

**Draft answer:** A Trooper must reveal to declare the Suppressive Fire Long Skill. Declaring a Long Skill other than Cautious Movement cancels Camouflaged State, so replace the Marker with its Model and then resolve Suppressive Fire if its requirements are met.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 158 — CAMOUFLAGED; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 171 — SUPPRESSIVE FIRE

## 2. Can a Trooper in Hidden Deployment place a Mine without revealing?

**Draft answer:** No. Declaring Place Deployable is an Order declaration, which cancels Hidden Deployment. Place the Trooper’s Model or eligible Marker at its recorded position, then resolve Place Deployable if its requirements are met.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 162 — HIDDEN DEPLOYMENT; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 82 — PLACE DEPLOYABLE

## 3. Can a Parachutist enter the table as part of a Coordinated Order?

**Draft answer:** Yes, if the Coordinated Order requirements are met. Off-table Parachutists may be selected to deploy as part of a Coordinated Order, but they lose the separate Order they would normally use to enter the table.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 105 — PARACHUTIST; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 129 — COORDINATED ORDER

## 4. Can a failed Doctor reroll be rerolled again with a Command Token?

**Draft answer:** Yes. After the built-in Doctor (ReRoll WIP=X) reroll fails, the Operational Use of a Command Token may still repeat the Doctor Roll. The WIP value printed in Doctor (ReRoll WIP=X) does not apply to that Command Token reroll.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 90 — DOCTOR; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 128 — COMMAND TOKENS

## 5. How many levels of Unconscious State does Engineer remove from a Remote Presence target?

**Draft answer:** A single successful Engineer Roll removes all Wounds necessary to cancel the target’s Unconscious State, regardless of which Remote Presence Unconscious level it occupied.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 91 — ENGINEER; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 91 — REMOTE PRESENCE

## 6. Does Mimetism protect a target from a Berserk CC Attack?

**Draft answer:** No. Mimetism does not apply to CC Attacks. Resolve Berserk using its CC rules and applicable CC MODs, without adding the target’s Mimetism MOD.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 102 — MIMETISM; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 86 — BERSERK

## 7. How is a Critical Intuitive Attack resolved against the Main Target and secondary Template targets?

**Draft answer:** The Critical applies only to the Main Target. Other Troopers affected by the Template treat the result as a normal successful Roll rather than a Critical.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 49 — INTUITIVE ATTACK; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 44 — AREA OF EFFECT OF TEMPLATE

## 8. Can Sixth Sense remove the Poor Visibility penalty created when an MSV user is attacked through White Noise?

**Draft answer:** Sixth Sense can let its user ignore the -6 MOD from the resulting Poor Visibility Zone when targeted through a Zero Visibility Zone. White Noise prevents the Multispectral Visor itself from reducing that MOD, so the benefit comes from Sixth Sense, not the visor.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 111 — SIXTH SENSE; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 61 — WHITE NOISE

## 9. Can Sensor reveal a Trooper in Hidden Deployment?

**Draft answer:** Yes. Sensor is explicitly capable of Discovering Hidden Deployment. A successful use cancels Hidden Deployment and places the Trooper as required by that State’s cancellation rules.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 111 — SENSOR; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 162 — HIDDEN DEPLOYMENT

## 10. Does Stealth prevent AROs when a Hacker moves through an enemy Repeater’s Hacking Area?

**Draft answer:** If the moving Trooper uses Cautious Movement, Idle, or a qualifying Basic Short Skill with the Movement Label, Stealth suppresses AROs from enemies without LoF, including through Hacking Area, unless an exception such as Sixth Sense applies. A non-qualifying declaration does not receive that protection.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 112 — STEALTH; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 56 — REPEATER

## 11. Can an Impersonation-1 Marker be Discovered without the usual WIP-3 penalty by a Biometric Visor user?

**Draft answer:** Yes. The Biometric Visor ignores the WIP-3 MOD imposed by Impersonation-1 when its user attempts the applicable Discover Roll.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 120 — BIOMETRIC VISOR; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 96 — IMPERSONATION

## 12. If a Lieutenant with Strategos converts Lieutenant Orders to Regular Orders, can those Orders be spent by the Combat Group?

**Draft answer:** Yes. At Order Count, Strategos replaces the applicable Special Lieutenant Orders with Regular Orders and adds them to the Lieutenant’s Combat Group Order Pool, where they are spent as Regular Orders.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 113 — STRATEGOS L1; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 17 — ORDER COUNT

## 13. Can a Peripheral and its Controller choose different targets when activated by the same Order?

**Draft answer:** Yes. They are activated by the same Order and declare the same sequence of Skills, but they are not required to choose the same target unless another rule imposes that restriction.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 106 — PERIPHERAL; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 27 — ORDER EXPENDITURE SEQUENCE

## 14. Which G: Jumper Proxy reacts if several Proxies gain an ARO from the same enemy Order?

**Draft answer:** The player chooses only one eligible Proxy to become the Active Proxy and places the Active Proxy Token beside it. The other Proxies do not also perform that ARO.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 93 — G: JUMPER; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 31 — ARO

## 15. What happens to a Minelayer weapon if its user fails a Superior Deployment Roll?

**Draft answer:** The Minelayer loses the Deployable weapon or Equipment in addition to the normal consequences of the failed deployment. If it has Disposable uses, subtract the lost item from the available uses.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 102 — MINELAYER; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 62 — SUPERIOR DEPLOYMENT

## 16. Can Explode be avoided by choosing not to activate it after entering Unconscious State?

**Draft answer:** No. Explode is Obligatory and triggers automatically when its activation condition is met. Resolve its listed effects, after which the user enters Dead State.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 92 — EXPLODE; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 172 — UNCONSCIOUS

## 17. If a Transmutation Trooper deploys as a Marker, what happens to the Marker when its profile changes?

**Draft answer:** Apply the Transmutation rule together with the Marker State’s replacement requirements. The Trooper changes to the applicable Unit Profile and the table representation must be updated to a legal Model, Marker, or State Token as the rules specify.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 117 — TRANSMUTATION; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 157 — CAMOUFLAGED

## 18. Can a Trooper use Cautious Movement while inside an enemy Hacker’s Hacking Area without generating an ARO?

**Draft answer:** A non-Hackable Trooper does not generate a Hacking ARO merely for beginning or ending Cautious Movement inside an enemy Hacking Area. A Hackable Trooper does generate AROs as usual in that situation.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 32 — CAUTIOUS MOVEMENT; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 55 — HACKING AREA

## 19. Does a TinBot continue providing its bonus after its owner enters Dead State?

**Draft answer:** No. The TinBot is removed when its owner enters Dead State, so its MODs or advantages no longer apply.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 127 — TINBOT; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 159 — DEAD

## 20. Can an enemy claim Partial Cover from another player’s Deployable Cover?

**Draft answer:** No. The Deployable Cover rule expressly prevents enemy Troopers from claiming Partial Cover from that Deployable Cover, even though it remains a Scenery Element for movement and positioning.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 122 — DEPLOYABLE COVER; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 41 — PARTIAL COVER
