## Canonical 4: What does Sixth Sense do?

### 13. Can you explain Sixth Sense in plain language?

**Proposed answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** Sixth Sense lets a Trooper respond to Attacks directed at it from outside its LoF, using a 360-degree LoF arc for that response. When declaring Dodge or Reset, it also ignores negative MODs except the listed Immobilized-A, Immobilized-B, and Isolated penalties. Enemy Stealth is ineffective against it.

## Canonical 43: Does declaring Dodge break Stealth?

### 169. Rules check: Does declaring Dodge break Stealth?

**Proposed answer — EVIDENCE-BOUNDED INTERPRETATION:** Yes, during the Stealth user's Active Turn. Dodge has the Movement Label, but it is a Short Skill/ARO—not a Basic Short Skill—so it does not satisfy Stealth's requirement. Enemies without LoF are therefore not prevented by Stealth from declaring an ARO. During the Reactive Turn, Stealth is not functioning, so there is no active Stealth effect to break.

## Canonical 59: If a Peripheral is disconnected during an Order, does it finish its declared Skill?

### 233. Rules check: If a Peripheral is disconnected during an Order, does it finish its declared Skill?

**Proposed answer — EVIDENCE-BOUNDED INTERPRETATION:** Usually yes, but the cause and Peripheral type matter. If an ordinary Peripheral disconnects because its Controller enters a Null or Isolated State, it enters Disconnected State at the end of that Order, after resolving its otherwise legal declaration. A failed required Coherency Check disconnects a Synchronized or Control Peripheral immediately before movement, so it does not continue that movement. A Cyberplug is a separate exception: when its Controller enters a Null or Isolated State, it switches to its Autonomous Profile at the end of the Order instead of entering Disconnected State.

**Sources:** infinity-rules-n5.3, p. 106 — PERIPHERAL; infinity-rules-n5.3, p. 107 — PERIPHERAL (CONTROL).

## Canonical 86: I shoot through Smoke with MSV1, the target shoots back, and the target is in Cover; what MODs apply?

### 341. Rules check: I shoot through Smoke with MSV1, the target shoots back, and the target is in Cover; what MODs apply?

**Proposed answer — EVIDENCE-BOUNDED INTERPRETATION:** For the MSV1 shooter's BS Attack, the return BS Attack makes that shooter the target of a BS Attack through the Smoke. The Smoke is therefore treated as Poor Visibility when the shooter draws LoF back, and MSV1 reduces that Visibility MOD from -6 to -3. Partial Cover adds another -3, so the shooter's known total is -6 before Range or other MODs. The target receives +3 on any Saving Roll from Partial Cover. For the target's return shot, being targeted through the Smoke also permits LoF by treating it as Poor Visibility, imposing -6 unless the target has a rule that reduces that MOD.
