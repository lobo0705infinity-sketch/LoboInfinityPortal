# Infinity Rules Expanded Benchmark — Readable Review

This document contains **400 candidate player phrasings** mapped to **100 approved canonical rulings**. Each ruling shows four questions, followed by the answer and sources once.


# DIRECT DEFINITION

## Ruling 1

**Players may ask:**

1. Can you explain Mimetism in plain language?
2. How does Mimetism work in Infinity N5.3?
3. What rules effects does Mimetism have?
4. Quick rules check: what exactly does Mimetism do?

**Answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** Mimetism imposes the Unit Profile's listed negative MOD, normally -3 or -6, on enemy BS Attacks that require LoF and on Discover Rolls against the user. It does not apply to CC Attacks.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 102 — MIMETISM.

**Answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** Dodge is a Short Skill/ARO using PH. It can oppose enemy Attacks in a Face to Face Roll; if successful, it can also provide Dodge movement and a change of facing, subject to the Skill's movement rules.

## Ruling 3

**Players may ask:**

10. How does Stealth work in Infinity N5.3?
11. What rules effects does Stealth have?
12. Quick rules check: what exactly does Stealth do?

**Answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** Stealth functions during the user's Active Turn. Cautious Movement, Idle, and Basic Short Skills with the Movement Label suppress AROs from enemies that lack LoF; declaring another Skill grants AROs normally. Stealth is ineffective against Sixth Sense.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 112 — STEALTH.

## Ruling 4

**Players may ask:**

13. Can you explain Sixth Sense in plain language?
14. How does Sixth Sense work in Infinity N5.3?
15. What rules effects does Sixth Sense have?
16. Quick rules check: what exactly does Sixth Sense do?

**Answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** Sixth Sense lets a Trooper respond to Attacks directed at it from outside its LoF, using a 360-degree LoF arc for that response. It ignores the -6 MOD from the resulting Poor Visibility Zone when targeted by a BS Attack through a Zero Visibility Zone. When declaring Dodge or Reset, it also ignores negative MODs except the listed Immobilized-A, Immobilized-B, and Isolated penalties. Enemy Stealth is ineffective against it.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 111 — SIXTH SENSE; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 112 — STEALTH.

# TWO RULE INTERACTION

## Ruling 21

**Players may ask:**

81. Rules check: Does Zero Pain suffer the Firewall MOD when used through an enemy Repeater?
82. In an Infinity N5.3 game, does Zero Pain suffer the Firewall MOD when used through an enemy Repeater?
83. How should we resolve this at the table: does Zero Pain suffer the Firewall MOD when used through an enemy Repeater?
84. Player wording 4: Does Zero Pain suffer the Firewall MOD when used through an enemy Repeater?

**Answer — EVIDENCE-BOUNDED INTERPRETATION:** No. Zero Pain does not suffer Firewall itself. Zero Pain imposes a −3 WIP MOD on the enemy Comms Attacks it opposes; Firewall likewise applies its MOD to an enemy Comms Attack made against the protected Trooper. When an enemy Hacker attacks through your Repeater, that attacking Hacker's Comms Attack receives the applicable Firewall MOD—the Zero Pain user's own Roll does not.

**Sources:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 55 — FIREWALLS; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 56 — REPEATER; Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 61 — ZERO PAIN.
