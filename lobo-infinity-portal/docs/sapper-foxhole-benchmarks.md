# Sapper and Foxhole benchmark assumption

Stationary Gunfighter and ARO evaluations assume profiles with Sapper have deployed in Foxhole State. They receive Mimetism (-3) against the opposing BS attack, including retaliation in an active-turn exchange. The existing benchmark cover assumption continues to apply.

This is a state assumption, not an addition to the official printed profile. Setting `foxholeState: false` models cancellation and removes only the Mimetism granted by Foxhole. Native Mimetism remains; modifiers do not stack, and native Mimetism (-6) stays stronger. MSV applies normally. Close-combat calculations receive no Mimetism modifier.

Source: https://infinitythewiki.com/Foxhole_State

The current official capture contains 17 exact affected profile identities: Acontecimento Regulars (1), Vystrel (9 across three armies), Zouaves (1), Haiduks (2), and Nikoul (4, including both physical profiles). The rule is skill-based and therefore also covers future Sapper profiles.

Rebuild Gunfighter and ARO catalogs, their compressed archives and chunks, the mobility-adjusted Gunfighter catalog, and portal ratings together. Vystrel is also part of the standard defensive suite, so other models' shooting ratings and relative ranks must be recalculated.
