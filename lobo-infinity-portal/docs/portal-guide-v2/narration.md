## Welcome

Welcome to the Lobo Infinity Portal. This walkthrough shows you how to find a game, prepare for it, submit your result, and explore the community.

## Dashboard

On the dashboard, Choose Your First Move takes you to Army Intelligence, a battle report, or events. Public browsing is open to everyone. Use Explore to find the portal’s other tools and directories.

## Navigation and page controls

The links above the page show where you are and let you return to a parent section. On pages with an artwork control, choose Show full artwork or Use compact artwork; your preference carries across supported pages. Check Data last updated when interpreting results. If a page reports a loading error, use its Retry button.

## Events

Select All Events in the sidebar. Open the event you want to join and check its registration status. For casual games, use the Game Network or find players on Discord.

## Standings

Use the event selector in the sidebar to choose your competition. The links below it lead to that event. In Standings, choose your division to see the players and recorded scores.

## Mission & Map

Before arranging a league game, open Mission and Map. Check the date range, the active missions, and the assigned maps. View Mission opens the scenario details from Mission Geist.

## Submit Game

After playing, select Submit Game. Choose the form that matches your game: League, Team Tournament, Casual, or Top Forty. Each button opens its own Google Form. Check your scores with your opponent before submitting.

The report shows the objective points, tournament points, and victory points. Scroll for the mission summary, map, highlights, and review. Battle stories are fictionalized; use the recorded results and player notes for the factual account.

## Army Intelligence

Open Army Intelligence. Choose a faction or sectorial, or try the Corregidor example. This page explores submitted lists, common profiles, specialist coverage, and battlefield roles.

## Known Army Lists

In Army Intelligence, select Known Army Lists to open the Army Lists page. The faction filter is already set to the faction or sectorial you were viewing. From here, search the submitted lists or narrow them by mission. Clear filters when you want to browse other armies.

## Army Lists on desktop

On a desktop, choose View List to open the roster in Corvus Belli Infinity Army. You can also open Army Lists directly from the sidebar. Use its search, faction, and mission filters to find a list, and select a column heading to sort the table.

Visual: Highlight View List and the search, faction and mission filters on the current desktop page.

## Army Lists on mobile

On a phone, the action is Copy Army Code. Tap it to copy the list’s code, then paste that code into Infinity Army’s import tool. Army Intelligence’s Known Army Lists link still takes you to the same faction-filtered Army Lists page.

## Rankings

Use Combat, Defense, Control, or All to browse tactical roles. Global benchmark rank compares a profile across all armies. In-faction benchmark rank compares it within the army you selected. Check the exact weapon and combat state, such as non-linked or Fireteam plus one special die. The benchmark rating, letter grade, and global percentile describe combat performance. Submitted-list popularity tells you how often players used that profile. Compare matching combat states, and read each rank’s total number of profiles.

## Factions

Open Factions to explore armies and sectorials through games recorded on the portal. Open an army’s card for its results and recent reports. Read the game count alongside the record and win rate. These numbers describe submitted games, rather than every game played by that faction.

## Players

Open Players to browse the community. Use the event filter for All Events or a specific competition. Each card shows a player’s division, game count, and record. Open a card to view their profile, then scroll to Game History to explore their recorded games.

## Missions

Open Missions and choose an event, or leave All Events selected. View Mission opens the available scenario details. Click a mission name for its analysis, including recorded games, first-turn win rate, faction performance, and recent battle reports. Read the number of games alongside each percentage.

## Lobo’s Little Helper

Open Lobo’s Little Helper for the updated guide and Discord link. In Discord, start with slash help. Use slash list analyse with an Army code for a readable roster, legality summary, and two D Tabletop Simulator export. Tactical Brief, Ratings, Classifieds, and T T S Notes open privately through buttons. Use slash combat matchup to compare two profiles, slash combat counters for reactive answers, and slash play for availability and game requests.
