# Infinity Rules New-Topic Expansion — Review Draft

Target: **400 new canonical rulings and 2,000 player phrasings**. Current completed batch: **20 rulings and 100 phrasings**. All remain pending review.

## 1. When can a Trooper use Alert?

**Players may ask:**

- When can a Trooper use Alert?
- Rules check: When can a Trooper use Alert?
- In Infinity N5.3, when can a Trooper use Alert?
- At the table, how should we resolve this: when can a Trooper use Alert?
- Quick version: When can a model use Alert?

**Draft answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** A Trooper may use Alert if it was not activated by an Order or ARO during that Order and either it or an allied Trooper in its Zone of Control was targeted by an Attack.

**Source:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 77 — ALERT!.

## 2. What does Alert let an eligible Trooper do?

**Players may ask:**

- What does Alert let an eligible Trooper do?
- Rules check: What does Alert let an eligible Trooper do?
- In Infinity N5.3, what does Alert let an eligible Trooper do?
- At the table, how should we resolve this: what does Alert let an eligible Trooper do?
- Quick version: What does Alert let an eligible model do?

**Draft answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** At the Conclusion of the Order, after Saving Rolls, an eligible Trooper may turn on the spot to change its LoF angle. This is automatic, requires no Roll, does not change position, and generates no ARO.

**Source:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 77 — ALERT!.

## 3. After failing to Discover a Marker, when may that same Trooper try again against it?

**Players may ask:**

- After failing to Discover a Marker, when may that same Trooper try again against it?
- Rules check: After failing to Discover a Marker, when may that same Trooper try again against it?
- In Infinity N5.3, after failing to Discover a Marker, when may that same Trooper try again against it?
- At the table, how should we resolve this: after failing to Discover a Marker, when may that same Trooper try again against it?
- Quick version: After failing to Discover a Marker, when may that same model try again against it?

**Draft answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** That Trooper cannot attempt to Discover the same Marker again until the next Player Turn. It may still Discover a different Marker, and a different friendly Trooper may attempt to Discover the original Marker.

**Source:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 77 — DISCOVER.

## 4. If a revealed Trooper enters Camouflaged or Impersonation State again, is it the same Marker for the failed-Discover restriction?

**Players may ask:**

- If a revealed Trooper enters Camouflaged or Impersonation State again, is it the same Marker for the failed-Discover restriction?
- Rules check: If a revealed Trooper enters Camouflaged or Impersonation State again, is it the same Marker for the failed-Discover restriction?
- In Infinity N5.3, if a revealed Trooper enters Camouflaged or Impersonation State again, is it the same Marker for the failed-Discover restriction?
- At the table, how should we resolve this: if a revealed Trooper enters Camouflaged or Impersonation State again, is it the same Marker for the failed-Discover restriction?
- Quick version: If a revealed model enters Camouflaged or Impersonation State again, is it the same Marker for the failed-Discover restriction?

**Draft answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** No. A Trooper that was revealed and later re-entered Camouflaged State or Impersonation-1 or Impersonation-2 State does not count as the same Marker for that restriction.

## 6. What happens if a declared Skill fails its Requirements at Resolution?

- In Infinity N5.3, what happens if a declared Skill fails its Requirements at Resolution?
- At the table, how should we resolve this: what happens if a declared Skill fails its Requirements at Resolution?
- Quick version: What happens if a declared Skill fails its Requirements at Resolution?

**Draft answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** The Trooper performs Idle instead. If the failed Skill used a Disposable weapon or piece of Equipment, the declared use is still expended; the Trooper also loses Camouflaged or Impersonation State if the failed declaration would normally cancel that State.

## 7. What does Look Out allow friendly Troopers to do?

**Players may ask:**

- What does Look Out allow friendly Troopers to do?
- Rules check: What does Look Out allow friendly Troopers to do?
- In Infinity N5.3, what does Look Out allow friendly Troopers to do?
- At the table, how should we resolve this: what does Look Out allow friendly Troopers to do?
- Quick version: What does Look Out allow friendly models to do?

**Draft answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** Look Out allows the declaring player's other Troopers to modify their LoF without moving. Each modification counts as that Trooper's ARO and is resolved with a Normal or Face to Face Dodge (PH-3) Roll as applicable.

**Source:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 81 — LOOK OUT!.

## 9. Where may a Trooper place a Deployable during the Active Turn?

- In Infinity N5.3, where may a Trooper place a Deployable during the Active Turn?
- At the table, how should we resolve this: where may a Trooper place a Deployable during the Active Turn?
- Quick version: Where may a model place a Deployable during the Active Turn?

**Draft answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** The Token or Model may be placed in Silhouette contact with the user or, if the user moved during the Order, in Silhouette contact with any part of the user's route. It must be fully supported and cannot be placed on a vertical surface.

## 13. What does Reload restore?

**Players may ask:**

- What does Reload restore?
- Rules check: What does Reload restore?
- In Infinity N5.3, what does Reload restore?
- At the table, how should we resolve this: what does Reload restore?
- Quick version: What does Reload restore?

**Draft answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** Without a Roll, Reload cancels the user's Unloaded State or restores all expended uses of its Disposable weapons and Equipment. Only one allied Trooper may receive this effect per Order.

## 19. What modifiers apply to a Speculative Attack?

**Players may ask:**

- What modifiers apply to a Speculative Attack?
- Rules check: What modifiers apply to a Speculative Attack?
- In Infinity N5.3, what modifiers apply to a Speculative Attack?
- At the table, how should we resolve this: what modifiers apply to a Speculative Attack?
- Quick version: What modifiers apply to a Speculative Attack?

**Draft answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** Apply the Speculative Attack MOD of -6 and the weapon's Range MOD. Do not apply other negative MODs such as Mimetism, Partial Cover, or Visibility Zones.

## 20. What are the Burst and LoF rules for Speculative Attack?

**Players may ask:**

- What are the Burst and LoF rules for Speculative Attack?
- Rules check: What are the Burst and LoF rules for Speculative Attack?
- In Infinity N5.3, what are the Burst and LoF rules for Speculative Attack?
- At the table, how should we resolve this: what are the Burst and LoF rules for Speculative Attack?
- Quick version: What are the Burst and LoF rules for Speculative Attack?

**Draft answer — EXPLICIT RULES ANSWER:** Speculative Attack is a Long Skill using a weapon with the Speculative Attack Trait. It may attack a target outside LoF, its Burst is always 1 regardless of the weapon or Burst MODs, and a valid trajectory to the impact point must exist.

**Source:** Infinity Rules N5.3, p. 48 — SPECULATIVE ATTACK.
