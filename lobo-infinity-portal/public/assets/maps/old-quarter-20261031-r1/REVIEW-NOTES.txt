OLD QUARTER EXCAVATION R1 - THE DIG
31 October 2026 - revised after terrain review

WHAT IS INCLUDED
An original urban excavation map, replacing Old Quarter Excavation's previous
terrain. Fourteen buildings/workshops/ruins, including four new compact utility
buildings and site offices. Main buildings are redistributed toward midfield.
Three excavation pockets have two access ramps each, continuous steel shoring
and timber supports. Detached intact walls and plain concrete cubes are gone.
Eight taller buildings have three states: roof on, upper floor exposed, ground
floor exposed. Six single-storey buildings have two states: roof on and ground
floor exposed. Roof ladders persist in every state. Building hover
tooltips report base-to-top height.

INSTALL
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a new folder.
2. Close TTS, then run Install.cmd. It backs up an existing Old Quarter bag and
   installs the map bag plus two separately named saves.
3. For a quick review, load "Old Quarter Excavation R1 - The Dig" under
   Games > Save and Load. This is a deployed terrain review save.
4. For a normal game, use your regular Infinity mod. In Objects > Saved Objects
   > Lobo Workshop - Current Maps, take the new Old Quarter Excavation bag and
   drop it on the usual map deployment spot.
5. "Lobo Workshop - Old Quarter Excavation R1" is the full 90-setup collection
   with exactly this one map replaced. Use this save when ready to update your
   existing Workshop collection. The original collection save is preserved.

If your TTS folder is elsewhere, run:
  Install.cmd "D:\your custom\Tabletop Simulator"

All runtime assets use publicly hosted, immutable URLs. No new Steam Cloud
asset uploads are needed. Source OBJ files and material textures are included
for future edits; the playable JSON files do not depend on local asset paths.

MISSION SETUP
48 x 48 inches, 300 points. Opposite corner deployment zones have a 24-inch
radius. Three 40 mm Consoles are placed at the centre and 16 inches in each
direction along the other diagonal. Six 25 mm Tech tokens start off the table
for normal player placement before army deployment.

Deployment opens checkboxes for 24-, 28- and 32-inch corner arcs. Select any
combination. Only 24 inches defines the mission Deployment Zones; 28/32 are
+4/+8-inch measuring aids and do not change Tech placement restrictions.
Mission opens the full mission reference, including scoring, setup, objective
actions, Specialist Troops, Operational Learning and point-level variants,
cross-checked against Mission Geist. Tech Guide toggles six green rings showing
one legal placement; players choose their own positions before army deployment.
The three Consoles reuse the familiar Micro Art Studio models and 40 mm bases.

Mission Geist reference:
https://infinitygeist.com/mission/s18_the_dig

The excavations are shallow open pits, about 1.25 inches below street level.
There are no tunnels. The OBJ export conversion has been corrected so the
ground openings and raised pavements align with the placed terrain.

One checked example, in table-centred (X,Z) inches:
  (-20,-20), (-20,-10), (-6,-6), (6,6), (20,10), (20,20)
The narrowest Tech-to-DZ edge clearance is 9.742 inches; the narrowest gap
between Tech-token edges is 9.016 inches. Both exceed the mission's 8 inches.

Official mission, including full scoring and Operational Learning:
https://experience.corvusbelli.com/en/game-content/16de25e7-8317-4d7c-8438-bd4284164855

VERIFICATION
- All 57 hosted mesh, collider and texture files match their local bytes.
- All visual meshes are below 25,000 expanded vertices; largest: 12,728.
- Compound collider pieces are below 256 triangles per component; locked
  non-convex colliders also remain below that limit.
- Checked complete legal six-Tech placement, Console clearances and connected
  S2 street routes from both diagonal deployment corners.
- Sampled 67 roof positions against three S2-height objective positions using
  actual terrain triangles. No sampled position saw all three Consoles.
- Import simulation checks all three pit cutouts/floors and fourteen building
  pavements. Checkbox tests cover all eight combinations, radius geometry,
  player-panel synchronization, save/load persistence and Mission content.
- Normal Infinity stage deployment and Clear Table pass for all root pieces
  and after switching taller buildings to state 2 or state 3.
- The replacement collection changes only bag 810340 and retains 90 setups.

The previews render the exported meshes; they are not TTS screenshots. Live
TTS import, physics, miniatures on every surface and gameplay balance still
need an in-game check before tournament use. The Windows installer has been
reviewed here but cannot be executed in this Linux environment.
