OLD QUARTER EXCAVATION R1 - THE DIG
31 October 2026 - revised board, floors, controls and rubble

THIS UPDATE
- All three excavation pockets are now 2.5 inches deep, with two longer
  diagonal access ramps each. A thicker, fully closed foundation supports the
  cuts and rests on the table; there is no open void underneath.
- The Mission brief's reported "pattern too complex" failure is fixed by
  replacing pattern-based text measurement with literal string searches.
  Requests made while UI is loading are queued and retried.
- Deployment has explicit text labels beside every checkbox, independent
  Side A (blue) and Side B (orange) switches, and 24/28/32-inch distances.
  A persistent colour legend identifies each side and its selected distances.
- Yellow mat dashes are removed. The mat now uses a unique photographic
  street finish with worn asphalt, coherent repairs, cracks and exposed cobbles.
- Custom Tech models remain removed. Ground floors remain separated from the
  mat; fourteen buildings and sixteen rubble clusters in three sizes remain.

THE MAP
48 x 48 inches, 300-point The Dig, diagonal corner deployment. Fourteen
buildings include four compact utility buildings/site offices and redistributed
main buildings. Eight taller buildings have roof / upper-floor / ground-floor
states. Six single-storey buildings have roof-on / roof-off states. External
ladders persist across states. Three familiar Micro Art Studio Consoles have
40 mm mission footprints at centre and +/-16 inches along the other diagonal.

INSTALL
1. Close TTS and extract the entire ZIP to a new folder.
2. Run Install.cmd. Existing copies of the Old Quarter bag and the two named
   review saves are backed up before replacement.
3. For a quick review: Games > Save and Load > Old Quarter Excavation R1 - The Dig.
4. For a normal game: use the new bag under Saved Objects > Lobo Workshop -
   Current Maps, then deploy it using the normal Infinity mod map spot.
5. The separately named Lobo Workshop - Old Quarter Excavation R1 save contains
   90 map bags with only Old Quarter replaced. Other map bags are unchanged.

For a custom TTS location:
  Install.cmd "D:\your custom\Tabletop Simulator"

CONTROLS AND MISSION
Mission opens the full mission reference: setup, scoring, objective actions,
Specialist Troops, Operational Learning, end conditions and point variants.
Deployment opens side switches and independent 24-, 28- and 32-inch arcs.
Show either side, both, or neither. Only
24 inches defines the mission Deployment Zones; 28/32 are +4/+8 measuring aids.
Tech Guide optionally shows one legal arrangement of six markers; it creates
no models and does not fix where players must place their Tech markers.

Reference: https://infinitygeist.com/mission/s18_the_dig
Official: https://experience.corvusbelli.com/en/game-content/16de25e7-8317-4d7c-8438-bd4284164855
