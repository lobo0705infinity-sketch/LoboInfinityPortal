CLOUDBREAK ARRAY R4.1
Aurora VII • Turn 5 • Network Node
Harmony City, Harmony Province

INSTALL ON WINDOWS
Extract the ZIP and double-click INSTALL-CLOUDBREAK-ARRAY.cmd. For a custom save location, drag your Tabletop Simulator data folder or its Saves folder onto the installer. It adds two numbered save slots, puts the deployable bag in Saved Objects, and opens the Lobo Maps Workshop page.

PLAY
Under Games > Save & Load, open Cloudbreak Array R4.1 - Network Node. The rooftop district is already deployed. Take one civilian per player from the HVTs bag and use two Modern Classified Objectives per player. Map Controls > Mission gives you the Network Node by Lobo link in a private field. Click the field, press Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C, then paste it into your browser. The ZIP also includes a double-clickable Network Node - MissionGeist shortcut. The full briefing is in the TTS Notebook.

EDIT THE CLOUDS
All 23 cloud banks start locked. Right-click the bank itself, open Toggles and turn Lock off. Drag it, or use the Gizmo to set its position, height or rotation. To remove it, select it and press Delete. Gravity is off for the banks, so you can leave one suspended where you place it. Save the table to keep the changes.

Map Controls > Map-specific options > Cloud boundaries traces each bank's current volume. The guide moves with its bank and disappears with it. The default layout keeps all 26 bridge decks clear.

AIR AND VISIBILITY
Open air between buildings is impassable. Only Troopers with Terrain (Zero-G) or Terrain (Total) may move through it. All Troopers may use the bridges.

Cloud banks are Zero Visibility Zones. The distant cloud deck is scenery. The five civilian airships are solid scenery. The Cirrus survey blimp crosses the view from Lift Exchange to Isobar Observatory. Rainband crest cuts another view of Isobar from Rainworks Stack. Greenhouse glazing allows Line of Fire.

DISTRICT BELOW
The 48-inch mat uses the exact foundation outlines of the 16 playable buildings. Their printed facades recede toward the streets, lower rooftops and courtyards of Harmony City. Printed streets, service decks and distant wisps are scenery. The 23 movable cloud banks are the marked Zero Visibility Zones. The upper municipal pump-room photo has been replaced in ten window bays by a weather instrument laboratory.

The bridges from Commuter Hall to Pump Six and Weather Records to Transit Arcade meet new entrances on the lowest surviving level. The old lower parallels between Courier Spire and North Canopy and between Lift Exchange and Aerostat Pier are gone. Both pairs retain their other crossing.

BUILDINGS AND OBJECTIVES
Each building is one 3.2-inch story shorter. Ten towers have furnished interiors and six are roof-only. In a building with states, State 1 exposes the lowest interior and the highest state shows the roof. Courier Spire has two states, its dispatch floor and its roof. Yellow ladders connect the levels. Pick up figures before changing a state that hides their floor. On each of the eight glazed greenhouses, a 25 mm base can pass along either wall or between the beds. Each aisle opens at both ends.

Right-click a console to record control after resolving its roll. Losing your last relay also loses control of the Primary Node. Each console appears with its supporting floor.

MAP CONTROLS
Map Rules covers open air, bridges and cloud visibility. Mission gives each player the scenario link. With the map in its starting orientation, deployment runs inward from the two opposite X edges. Normal, +4 and +8 mark 12, 16 and 20 inches from each edge. The dotted exclusion lines mark the 8-inch band in the center. The controls also include Turn 180, independent DZ and exclusion colors, grid, quadrants, reference tables, Clear Table and Day / Night / Off.
