# Gaugamela Save 435 repair 1

Standalone model and texture assets for both Gaugamela layouts. Prior Granicus and Zama repairs are retained in the downloadable save. No other battle bag is changed.

New three-pose Persian spearmen and archers, with matching casualties, and a four-horse scythed chariot. Artwork generated with OpenAI image generation and packed without resizing its source pixels. Period reference: https://www.namuseum.gr/en/monthly_artefact/barbarian-clothing-or-the-garments-of-the-foreigners/ and https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/clothing-ii/ . Illustrative reconstructions, not archaeological replicas.

66 model/state configurations independently checked for intersecting figure bounds, coincident triangles, terrain clearance and whole-base containment. Distinct asset payloads may be shared. Full reports, source scripts, prompts and preview renders are in the installer Build-notes folder.

Release candidate: offline checks only. Live Tabletop Simulator, Windows installation and multiplayer remain untested. Steam Workshop is not updated by this commit.
